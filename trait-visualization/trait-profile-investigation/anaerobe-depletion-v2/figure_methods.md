# Figure Methods

These method notes describe how each PNG in this investigation folder was generated by `scripts/investigate_gut_anaerobe_depletion.py`.

## Shared Inputs

Study-profile z-scores were read from `trait-visualization/study_trait_profile_clustered_heatmap_matrix.tsv` and joined to study metadata from `trait-visualization/study_trait_profile_study_metadata.tsv`. The anaerobe v2 depletion guild score was the row-wise mean of the selected oxygen-tolerance counterpart traits: anaerobic and obligately anaerobic. This makes the comparison parallel to the facultative-aerobe v2 guild, with the focus direction switched to decreased profiles. Profiles from all body sites are retained, and body-site summaries are used to separate gut, oral, airway, urogenital, and other contexts.
`guild_trait_selection.tsv` documents the a priori selected trait set, the focus/comparator directions, a short selection justification for each trait, and KG-Microbe grounding as the count of taxa carrying each trait in `data/derived/taxa_trait_matrix.tsv`.
Direction labels (`increased` and `decreased`) were taken from BugSigDB's curated `Abundance in Group 1` signature field. This script did not recalculate differential abundance; it grouped and filtered BugSigDB-reported study/experiment/direction profiles by condition and body-site metadata.

The focal taxa were defined a priori in the script's `TARGET_TAXA` map using NCBI Taxonomy identifiers, which avoids ambiguity from synonym or strain-name differences. The selected taxa were Faecalibacterium prausnitzii (NCBITaxon:853), Anaerobutyricum hallii (NCBITaxon:39488), Lachnospira eligens (NCBITaxon:39485), Agathobacter rectalis (NCBITaxon:39491), Roseburia intestinalis (NCBITaxon:166486), Ruminococcus bromii (NCBITaxon:40518), and Roseburia inulinivorans (NCBITaxon:360807). These taxa are fixed focal examples for recurrence audits in decreased profiles; they do not define the formal v2 guild score. The formal guild is the opposite-direction oxygen-tolerance trait set described above, and the derived taxa-trait matrix gives each selected focal taxon at least one of those investigation guild-trait annotations. The script writes `candidate_taxa_trait_presence.tsv` from `data/derived/taxa_trait_matrix.tsv` as the audit trail for those trait annotations.

## `candidate_taxa_depletion_bias.png`

Guild-specificity: no. This is a fixed target-taxon direction-count view; it is not recomputed from the v2 guild score.

This figure uses the fixed focal taxa listed above, then intersects their NCBI Taxonomy IDs with `trait-visualization/taxa_guild_discovery_taxa_metadata.tsv`. That metadata table supplies the total BugSigDB profile count and the number of profiles where each taxon was reported as increased or decreased. For each target taxon, depletion bias was calculated as `(decreased profile count - increased profile count) / total BugSigDB profile count`. The horizontal bars show this depletion-bias value for each target taxon. No condition or body-site filter is applied to this figure; it summarizes the overall BugSigDB direction tendency of the preselected focal taxa.

## `condition_decreased_guild_signal.png`

Guild-specificity: yes. This figure uses the anaerobe v2 depletion guild score from the two selected traits.

Study profiles from all body sites were grouped by BugSigDB condition label. Groups with fewer than five profiles were excluded. Broad non-condition or metadata labels (`Body fat distribution`, `Clinical treatment`, `Exposure`, `Health trait`, `Microbiome`, `Population`, `Physical activity`, and labels containing `exposure`, `measurement`, or `population`) were removed from the displayed condition summary. The figure keeps the top 12 eligible conditions with at least three decreased profiles, at least two studies, positive decreased-minus-increased guild signal, and positive decreased-profile mean guild z-score, using fallback filtering if needed. Conditions are selected and sorted by decreased-minus-increased guild signal, and bar length is this enrichment-oriented contrast. The right-side labels show the paired standardized mean difference (`paired d`) calculated from matched decreased/increased profiles within the same study and experiment, plus a two-sided paired t-test p-value for the same matched differences. `paired d=NA` means fewer than three matched profile pairs, or zero variance among the paired differences; in those cases the paired p-value is also `NA`.

## `guild_trait_direction_shift.png`

Guild-specificity: yes. This figure shows the two selected v2 guild traits and their decreased-minus-increased profile shifts.

For each selected guild trait, the script calculated the mean z-score in decreased study profiles and the mean z-score in increased study profiles. Bars show `decreased mean z - increased mean z`; positive values indicate traits that are higher in decreased profiles.

## `anatomical_sites_diagram.png`

Guild-specificity: yes. Color encodes the anaerobe v2 depletion guild score among decreased profiles at each body-site group.

Profiles were grouped by `body_site_group`, requiring at least 10 profiles per group. The display excludes the generic `Other` body-site group. Bubble and bar length encode the number of study profiles in the body-site group. Color encodes the mean anaerobe v2 depletion guild z-score among decreased profiles at that body site. Body-site labels were placed on a simplified, neutral front-view body outline using fixed anatomical coordinates for feces/stool, colon, caecum, oral, airway, urogenital, and unknown labels.

## `target_taxa_condition_breakdown_bar.png`

Guild-specificity: no. This is a fixed target-taxon occurrence-count view; it is not recomputed from the v2 guild score.

Target-taxon occurrences were loaded from `data/derived/taxa.tsv`, limited to the fixed focal NCBI Taxonomy IDs, deduplicated by taxon and study profile, and joined to the scored study profiles. This produces one row per focal taxon/profile occurrence with condition, body site, direction, and guild-score metadata. The bar chart counts decreased target-taxon/profile occurrences by condition after removing the broad non-condition and metadata labels described above. A condition with multiple focal taxa can therefore contribute multiple occurrences; the chart is an occurrence breakdown, not a unique-study breakdown. It displays the top 20 explicit condition labels as horizontal bars, with bar labels showing occurrence counts.

## Inferred Guild Evidence TSVs

`kg_microbe_candidate_taxon_universe.tsv`, `condition_inferred_guild_summary.tsv`, `condition_inferred_guild_trait_evidence.tsv`, and `condition_inferred_guild_taxa_evidence.tsv` provide the anaerobe-side match to the facultative-aerobe inferred evidence outputs. The taxon universe is first defined across all taxa in `data/derived/taxa_trait_matrix.tsv`: a taxon is included when it has at least one core oxygen-tolerance annotation among anaerobic or obligately anaerobic. For each top decreased-profile condition, the script intersects that KG-Microbe-wide universe with taxa actually observed in the condition's decreased profiles, identifies v2 guild traits whose z-score is above zero in the decreased direction and whose decreased-minus-increased condition contrast is positive, and reports which universe taxa carry those enriched guild-trait annotations.
