# Six Shared Traits Interpretations

## Overall Interpretation

The grouped Cytoscape diagrams show that the six shared traits are not disease-specific. They recur across broad disease domains, especially infectious disease, cancer/neoplasia, metabolic/endocrine disease, neurologic/cognitive disease, colorectal neoplasia, respiratory/airway disease, and reproductive/hormonal health. This suggests the six traits are behaving like cross-condition microbial phenotype signals rather than isolated findings.

The individual-condition diagrams clarify which diseases are driving those grouped patterns. The strongest repeated disease-level signals are Parkinson's disease, colorectal cancer, COVID-19, obesity, human papilloma virus infection, gastric cancer, and dry eye syndrome. Several of these show all six traits in both increased and decreased views, especially Parkinson's disease, colorectal cancer, COVID-19, HPV infection, gastric cancer, and dry eye syndrome.

## Increased vs Decreased

The increased diagrams are generally stronger and denser: grouped increased profiles have `n=4,495` positive-z memberships versus `n=3,795` for decreased; individual increased has `n=1,645` versus `n=1,434` decreased.

Most traits are stronger in increased profiles: facultatively aerobic, coccus shaped, sphere shaped, arsenate reduction, and moderately halophilic. The main exception is cellulose catabolic process, which is comparatively more prominent in decreased profiles. This matches the paired trait statistics, where cellulose catabolic process is the only one of the six selected traits enriched in decreased profiles.

Feces dominates both directions, but it is stronger in increased profiles overall. In grouped diagrams, feces contributes `2,857` increased memberships versus `2,260` decreased. The largest increased-dominant fecal signals are facultatively aerobic, moderately halophilic, arsenate reduction, coccus shaped, and sphere shaped. In contrast, fecal cellulose catabolic process is decreased-dominant.

Oral cavity/dental is one of the clearest decreased-skewed body sites: `396` increased versus `478` decreased in grouped diagrams. Ocular/eye, intestinal/GI mucosa, and stomach/esophagus lean more increased.

## Grouped vs Individual

Grouped diagrams are best for seeing broad disease-domain structure. They show that the same six traits span many categories at once, with infectious disease, cancer/neoplasia, metabolic/endocrine, neurologic/cognitive, and colorectal neoplasia repeatedly carrying all six traits.

Individual diagrams are better for identifying the specific disease anchors behind those broad groups. For example, the grouped neurologic/cognitive signal is strongly represented by Parkinson's disease; metabolic/endocrine by obesity; infectious disease by COVID-19 and HPV infection; cancer/neoplasia by colorectal, gastric, cervical, ovarian, and squamous cell carcinoma signals.

The individual diagrams are sparser because they retain raw disease labels and fewer body-site nodes, but the same high-level pattern remains: multi-trait signals recur across multiple diseases and body sites rather than being limited to one condition.

## Cross-Disease Patterns

The most consistent cross-disease pattern is a fecal multi-trait signature spanning neurologic, metabolic, colorectal/GI, infectious, and diet-related conditions. Parkinson's disease and colorectal cancer are the strongest examples.

There are also site-specific multi-disease patterns: COVID-19 links the six traits across upper airway/nasal and feces; HPV infection links vaginal/reproductive and oral/dental sites; gastric cancer concentrates in stomach/esophagus; dry eye syndrome and glaucoma concentrate in ocular/eye.

Taken together, the diagrams suggest a shared microbial phenotype axis across diseases: aerotolerance/oxygen exposure, coccoid or spherical morphology, halophily/osmotic tolerance, arsenate reduction, and cellulose metabolism recur across disease contexts, but their directionality differs. Increased profiles emphasize the broader aerobic/coccus/sphere/halophilic/arsenate pattern, while decreased profiles particularly elevate cellulose catabolic process and oral/dental-associated signals.
