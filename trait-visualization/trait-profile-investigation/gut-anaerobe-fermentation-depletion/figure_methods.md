# Figure Methods

These method notes describe how each PNG in this investigation folder was generated by `scripts/investigate_gut_anaerobe_depletion.py`.

## Shared Inputs

Study-profile z-scores were read from `trait-visualization/study_trait_profile_clustered_heatmap_matrix.tsv` and joined to study metadata from `trait-visualization/study_trait_profile_study_metadata.tsv`. The gut anaerobe/fermentation guild score was the row-wise mean of the selected guild traits: anaerobic, obligately anaerobic, Fermentation, cellulose catabolic process, and xylan catabolic process. Gut-context profiles were defined by body-site or grouped body-site labels matching `feces`, `faeces`, `stool`, `colon`, `caecum`, `cecum`, `gut`, `intestin`, or `digestive`.

The focal taxa were defined a priori in the script's `TARGET_TAXA` map using NCBI Taxonomy identifiers, which avoids ambiguity from synonym or strain-name differences. The selected taxa were Faecalibacterium prausnitzii (NCBITaxon:853), Anaerobutyricum hallii (NCBITaxon:39488), Lachnospira eligens (NCBITaxon:39485), Agathobacter rectalis (NCBITaxon:39491), Roseburia intestinalis (NCBITaxon:166486), Ruminococcus bromii (NCBITaxon:40518), and Roseburia inulinivorans (NCBITaxon:360807). They were chosen as focal examples for the gut anaerobe/fermentation depletion hypothesis because they are species-level gut anaerobe or fiber-fermentation candidates in the selected guild, and the derived taxa-trait matrix gives each selected taxon at least one of the investigation guild-trait annotations. The script writes `candidate_taxa_trait_presence.tsv` from `data/derived/taxa_trait_matrix.tsv` as the audit trail for those trait annotations.

## `candidate_taxa_depletion_bias.png`

This figure uses the fixed focal taxa listed above, then intersects their NCBI Taxonomy IDs with `trait-visualization/taxa_guild_discovery_taxa_metadata.tsv`. That metadata table supplies the total BugSigDB profile count and the number of profiles where each taxon was reported as increased or decreased. For each target taxon, depletion bias was calculated as `(decreased profile count - increased profile count) / total BugSigDB profile count`. The horizontal bars show this depletion-bias value for each target taxon. No condition or body-site filter is applied to this figure; it summarizes the overall BugSigDB direction tendency of the preselected focal taxa.

## `condition_decreased_guild_signal.png`

Gut-context study profiles were grouped by BugSigDB condition label. Groups with fewer than five profiles were excluded, and the broad label `Clinical treatment` was removed from the displayed condition summary. The figure keeps the top 12 eligible conditions with at least three decreased profiles and at least two studies, using fallback filtering if needed. Bar height represents the mean gut anaerobe/fermentation guild z-score among decreased profiles for that condition.

## `guild_trait_direction_shift.png`

For each selected guild trait, the script calculated the mean z-score in all decreased study profiles and the mean z-score in all increased study profiles. Bars show `decreased mean z - increased mean z`; positive values indicate traits that are higher in decreased profiles.

## `anatomical_sites_diagram.png`

Gut-context profiles were grouped by `body_site_group`, requiring at least 10 profiles per group. The display excludes the generic `Other` body-site group. Bubble and bar length encode the number of study profiles in the body-site group. Color encodes the mean gut anaerobe/fermentation guild z-score among decreased profiles at that body site. Body-site labels were placed on a simplified, neutral front-view body outline using fixed anatomical coordinates for feces/stool, colon, caecum, oral, airway, urogenital, and unknown labels.

## `target_taxa_condition_breakdown_pie.png`

Target-taxon occurrences were loaded from `data/derived/taxa.tsv`, limited to the fixed focal NCBI Taxonomy IDs, deduplicated by taxon and study profile, and joined to the scored study profiles. This produces one row per focal taxon/profile occurrence with condition, body site, direction, and guild-score metadata. The pie chart counts decreased target-taxon/profile occurrences in gut-context profiles by condition, after removing `Clinical treatment`. A condition with multiple focal taxa can therefore contribute multiple occurrences; the chart is an occurrence breakdown, not a unique-study breakdown. The top 12 condition labels are shown directly; all remaining labels are collapsed into `other conditions`. Percent labels below 2% are hidden for readability.

## `target_taxa_condition_breakdown_histogram.png`

The histogram uses the same decreased gut-context focal-taxon/profile condition counts as the pie chart, including the same target-taxon selection, gut-context filter, deduplication rule, and `Clinical treatment` exclusion. It displays the top 20 explicit condition labels as horizontal bars, with bar labels showing occurrence counts.
