# Exploratory PD Taxa-by-Six-Trait Influence Heatmap

![Heatmap](pd_exploratory_taxa_six_traits_influence_heatmap.png)

This is the expanded companion to the strict species-only influence heatmap. It includes reported PD fecal taxa at species, genus, and family rank, uses a lower exploratory support threshold of 2 studies, and caps the display at 30 rows so the figure stays readable. Rows are selected by keeping DOI-backed PD literature matches first, then adding the best-supported non-literature rank roll-ups. The y-axis then groups the selected taxa into species, genus, and family blocks in that order; dark horizontal rules separate the rank blocks.

*Mediterraneibacter torques* is explicitly excluded. Only cell values greater than +0.01 or less than -0.01 are drawn, and rows with no value beyond that threshold are omitted. The cell TSV retains all six exact estimates for each displayed taxon and marks plotted cells with `displayed_in_heatmap`.

The displayed figure contains 30 taxa out of 51 eligible species/genus/family rows, 25 of which match the curated PD taxa reference. It has 6 traits and 180 taxon-trait cells. Cell status counts: {'mixed': 161, 'low support': 12, 'dec': 6, 'inc': 1}.

Use this view to find candidate taxa and rank-level patterns that the conservative species-only heatmap may miss. Treat it as hypothesis-generating: genus and family rows can mix multiple species, and the relaxed support threshold is meant to reveal leads, not final directional claims. The x-axis `baseline Δz` is the full-data paired trait contrast before taxon removal; each cell is baseline Δz minus the recomputed contrast after removing that row taxon. These non-additive influence values show whether a taxon changes the trait contrast, not whether it causally produces the trait effect. Column arrows and `lollipop` values give each trait's observed direction in the PD six-trait lollipop, the sign of `increased_minus_decreased_mean_z` in `pd_six_trait_direction_summary.tsv`; the trait and cell tables carry it as `trait_direction`.
