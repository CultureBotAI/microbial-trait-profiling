| R05 Arsenate reduction means *arsC* detoxification / plasmid passenger | `METPO:2000103\|pathways:arsenate_reduction` does not identify specific genes (*arsC* vs. *arrAB*), plasmids, or heavy metal co-selection. | R06 Moderately halophilic indicates halotolerance / osmotolerance | METPO:1000623 defines an explicit requirement for $\ge 0.2$ M NaCl (halophily), not osmotolerance without salt requirement. | R07 Cellulose signal indicates SCFA loss or impaired cross-feeding | `METPO:2000103\|GO:0030245` annotates pathway capacity, not in vivo degradation, metabolite flux, or butyrate production. Primary cellulolytic microbes ferment cellulose to acetate and succinate, not butyrate. | #189 / #191 |
| R08 Dominant genera drive the signal; exclusions should weaken it | Falsifiable predictions, not assumed findings. Reconcile all trait contributions, allow null or opposite outcomes, and evaluate sensitivity without treating exclusions as causal interventions. | #190 |

Disease labels and exclusion flags are not measured inflammation or exposure. | #191 |
