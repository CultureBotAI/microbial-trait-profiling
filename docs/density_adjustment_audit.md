## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

Discussions in [#264](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/microbial-trait-profiling/issues/264) and [#118](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/microbial-trait-profiling/issues/118) raised a fundamental methodological question:
> *Is the dense positive correlation across microbial traits a biological signal of co-selected phenotypes, or is it an artifact of annotation density driven by uneven curation effort across well-studied model organisms?*

To resolve this question empirically:
1. We quantify the baseline trait correlation structure across all non-MediaDive traits and measure the variance explained by the primary principal component (PC1).
2. We test whether the correlation structure collapses or persists after adjusting for the primary annotation density axis using:
   - **Method A:** Profile-level PC1 regression / residualization.
   - **Method B:** Profile composition normalization (Total Sum Scaling / profile annotation richness normalization: $x_i / \sum_j x_{ij}$).
3. We test the explicit diagnostic case identified in #264: *facultatively aerobic* (`METPO:1000608`) $\times$ *moderately halophilic* (`METPO:1000623`).
4. We evaluate the survival of the six canonical Table 1 convergent traits under density adjustment.
5. We audit near-twin candidate features (*coccus-shaped* `METPO:1000668` vs. *sphere-shaped* `METPO:1000683`) for bivariate correlation, partial correlation controlling for study annotation depth, paired contrast correlation, and Variance Inflation Factor (VIF).

## 2. Baseline Dimensionality & Correlation Structure

Restricting the trait universe to non-MediaDive traits (`object_category != 'METPO:1004005'`) yields $P = 317$ trait features across $N = 12,108$ study trait profiles ($0$ constant columns excluded). Note that exact correlation figures depend on the profile representation (raw count sums vs. binarized presence/absence per #365); the audit evaluates the wide profile representations generated by the pipeline.

| Metric | Pearson Correlation | Spearman Correlation ($\rho$) | Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Median Pairwise Correlation** | $+0.7065$ | $+0.6117$ | Pervasive positive correlation across trait pairs. |
| **Mean Pairwise Correlation** | $+0.6044$ | $+0.5497$ | Substantial global positive shift. |
| **Pairs Exceeding $+0.5$** | 68.0% | 63.1% | Majority of trait pairs exhibit strong positive correlation. |
| **Pairs Exceeding $+0.9$** | 18.2% | 3.4% | Substantial number of near-redundant pairs in raw counts. |
| **PC1 Variance Explained** | **66.80%** | **60.50%** | A single global axis accounts for over 66% of total trait variation. |
| **PC1–PC5 Variance Explained** | **89.35%** | — | Top 5 components explain $\approx 89\%$ of total variance. |

### Effective Number of Independent Tests ($M_{\text{eff}}$)

Treating traits as independent tests in statistical inference severely over-corrects (Bonferroni $\alpha = 0.05 / P$). We evaluate spectral estimators of $M_{\text{eff}}$ from the eigenvalues of the correlation matrix:

- **Li & Ji (2005):** Canonical estimator in `bsdbng.trait_screen.effective_family_size`  
  $$f(\lambda) = \mathbb{I}(|\lambda| \ge 1) + (|\lambda| - \lfloor |\lambda| \rfloor)$$
  *(Uses absolute eigenvalues $|\lambda|$ and excludes constant columns to prevent numerical roundoff artifacts from inflating $M_{\text{eff}}$ per #352, #377, #378).*
  **$M_{\text{eff}} = 29.0$**
- **Galwey (2009):** Alternative spectral estimator  
  $$M_{\text{eff}} = \frac{\left(\sum \sqrt{\max(0, \lambda_i)}\right)^2}{\sum \lambda_i}$$
  **$M_{\text{eff}} = 16.4$**
- **Cheverud (1995) / Nyholt (2004):** Excluded as unreliable per #325 and #357 ($M_{\text{eff}} = 171.4$) due to severe sensitivity to collinear eigenvalue distributions and failure to satisfy basic boundary constraints in empirical genomic matrices.

Estimators confirm that the effective dimensionality of the 317-trait space is on the order of $\approx 16$ to $29$ independent dimensions, driven by the concentration of variance on PC1.

## 3. Density Axis Adjustment: Method Comparison

### Method A: PC1 Regression Residualization
We standardize each trait column and regress out the first principal component scores:
$$\mathbf{x}_j^{\text{adj}} = \mathbf{x}_j - \hat{\beta}_j \mathbf{pc}_1$$
- **Effect on global correlation:** The median pairwise correlation collapses from $+0.7065$ to **$-0.0357$** (centered around zero), and mean pairwise correlation drops to $+0.0013$.
- The proportion of pairs with $r > 0.5$ drops from $68.0\%$ to $17.3\%$.
- Residual traits are orthogonal to the global curation density component.

### Method B: Profile Composition Normalization (Total Sum Scaling)
Each profile's trait counts are normalized by its row sum across non-MediaDive traits:
$$x_{ij}^{\text{norm}} = \frac{x_{ij}}{\sum_k x_{ik}}$$
This adjusts for overall profile annotation richness and study annotation depth (#352, #379).
- **Effect on diagnostic pair correlation:** Reduces Pearson correlation between *facultatively aerobic* and *moderately halophilic* from $+0.8990$ to **$+0.1442$**.
- Method B substantially attenuates curation bias while preserving relative compositional shifts. Method A provides complete orthogonal separation from PC1.

## 4. Diagnostic Test Case: Facultatively Aerobic $\times$ Moderately Halophilic

Issue #264 posed the diagnostic question: *Does the observed correlation between facultatively aerobic (`METPO:1000608`) and moderately halophilic (`METPO:1000623`) reflect true biological co-occurrence or annotation density?*

- **Raw Pairwise Correlation:** Pearson $r = +0.8990$.
- **Post-PC1 Regression:** Pearson $r = +0.6053$ (an attenuation of $\Delta r = -0.2937$).
- **Post-Profile-Composition:** Pearson $r = +0.1442$ (an attenuation of $\Delta r = -0.7548$).
- **Paired Experiment Contrasts ($N = 4,784$):**
  - **Raw Difference:** Wilcoxon signed-rank $p = 1.82 \times 10^{-11}$ (significant).
  - **Density-Adjusted Difference:** Wilcoxon signed-rank $p = 1.39 \times 10^{-8}$ (remains significant post-adjustment in raw count profiles).

**Finding:** The correlation between *facultatively aerobic* and *moderately halophilic* is substantially inflated by the primary density axis, attenuating by $\Delta r = -0.29$ under PC1 regression and dropping to $+0.14$ under profile composition normalization. While the paired contrast survives in raw count profiles, previous analyses under binarized profiles showed full attenuation (#375), demonstrating sensitivity to profile representation.

## 5. Survival of Convergent Six Trait Signals

We evaluated whether genuine biological signals survive PC1 density adjustment in paired experiment comparisons (increased vs. decreased, $N = 4,784$ pairs):

| Trait Identifier | Trait Label | Raw Mean $\Delta$ | Raw Wilcoxon $p$ | Adj Mean $\Delta$ | Adj Wilcoxon $p$ | Relative Change | Adjustment Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `METPO:1000608` | Facultatively aerobic | $+229.70$ | $1.82 \times 10^{-11}$ | $+123.53$ | **$1.39 \times 10^{-8}$** | -46.2% | **Survived (significant post-adjustment)** |
| `METPO:2000103\|GO:0030245` | Cellulose catabolic process | $-2.58$ | $1.83 \times 10^{-4}$ | **$-4.83$** | **$6.88 \times 10^{-11}$** | +87.6% | **Enhanced (significant, larger magnitude)** |
| `METPO:1000668` | Coccus shaped | $+74.07$ | $4.96 \times 10^{-4}$ | $-12.52$ | **$5.76 \times 10^{-1}$** | -116.9% | **Collapsed (attenuated to nonsignificant)** |
| `METPO:1000683` | Sphere shaped | $+4.85$ | $2.66 \times 10^{-3}$ | $-0.16$ | **$5.52 \times 10^{-1}$** | -103.3% | **Collapsed (attenuated to nonsignificant)** |
| `METPO:2000103\|pathways:arsenate_reduction` | Arsenate reduction | $+1.63$ | $4.04 \times 10^{-8}$ | $+0.86$ | **$5.70 \times 10^{-4}$** | -47.0% | **Survived (significant post-adjustment)** |
| `METPO:1000623` | Moderately halophilic | $+9.22$ | $4.68 \times 10^{-8}$ | $+3.26$ | **$3.72 \times 10^{-10}$** | -64.7% | **Survived (significant post-adjustment)** |

### Key Methodological Takeaways
1. **Cellulose Catabolism Depletion Is Strengthened:** When the positive curation baseline is removed, the depletion of cellulose-degrading bacteria in disease signatures becomes substantially sharper ($p = 6.88 \times 10^{-11}$). In raw data, the global positive density drift masked the true magnitude of cellulose depletion.
2. **Morphological Signals Attenuate to Nonsignificance:** *Coccus shaped* ($p = 0.576$) and *sphere shaped* ($p = 0.552$) collapse to nonsignificance after removing PC1, demonstrating that their raw enrichment signal was driven by the shared curation density component.
3. **Physiological Signals Survive with Attenuated Magnitude:** *Facultatively aerobic*, *moderately halophilic*, and *arsenate reduction* maintain statistical significance in raw count profiles, though their mean effect magnitudes attenuate by 46–65%.
4. **Representation Sensitivity (#375):** The survival versus collapse of specific traits (e.g., *facultatively aerobic* and *arsenate reduction*) is sensitive to profile representation (raw count sums vs. binarized presence/absence per #365 opt-in flags). Pipelines should report both representations.

## 6. Near-Twin Collinearity & Conditional Testing (Coccus vs. Sphere)

We evaluated the collinearity and conditional independence of the sibling morphological traits *coccus-shaped* (`METPO:1000668`) and *sphere-shaped* (`METPO:1000683`):

- **Profile Correlation:** $r = +0.7969$
- **Partial Correlation (controlling for annotation depth):** $r = +0.4736$
- **Variance Inflation Factor (VIF):** $2.74$ (paired contrast VIF = $2.29$)
- **Profile Regression Slope (Coccus ~ Sphere):** $\beta = 20.29$
- **Paired Regression Slope:** $\beta = 22.06$ ($p < 10^{-15}$)

### Methodological Recommendations for Near-Twins
- **Substantial Collinearity:** An $r \approx 0.80$ and VIF of $2.74$ demonstrate substantial collinearity between sibling morphological descriptors.
- **Controlled Conditioning:** The traits retain partial correlation ($r = 0.4736$) after conditioning on total profile annotation depth, confirming that their association is terminological/ontological rather than an artifact of total annotation count alone.
- **Annotation Disparity:** The steep regression slope ($\beta \approx 22$) reflects the substantial annotation disparity between the two terms across distinct source ontologies (`infores:madin_etal` vs. `infores:bactotraits`, per #189).
- **Recommendation:** Analysts should not include both *coccus-shaped* and *sphere-shaped* as independent unregularized predictors in the same multivariate model. Sibling morphological terms should be consolidated, regularized (Ridge/ElasticNet), or evaluated with conditional contrasts.

## 7. Biological Interpretation Bounds

Following the project interpretation guidelines in `AGENTS.md` and [#179](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/microbial-trait-profiling/issues/179):
1. **Annotation Representation Only:** Trait counts measure curated knowledge representation in published signatures, not physical abundance in patient microbiomes.
2. **Do Not Conflate Density with Co-selection:** Positive correlations across traits in raw profiles must not be cited as biological co-selection or shared operon architecture without testing against PC1 density residualization.
3. **No Causal Inferences:** The survival of *moderately halophilic* ($p = 3.72 \times 10^{-10}$) does not indicate patient-level osmolarity changes or causal dysbiosis; it indicates that taxa curated with this trait are disproportionately represented in published disease-increased signatures.
